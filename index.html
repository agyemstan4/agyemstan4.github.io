About Me

Engineer by degree, builder by nature

I'm a recent BEng Software Engineering graduate from De Montfort University, targeting a first-class degree. My main focus is Android — I've spent the past few years building full-stack mobile apps independently, owning everything from system architecture through to testing on real hardware.

My flagship project VibeNSync is a social music intelligence app built with Kotlin, Firebase, and the Spotify API — featuring real-time synced playback, a friend system with compatibility scoring, and mood-based playlist generation. It's the kind of project that taught me more than any module could.

Outside of my main stack I'm building passive income projects: Android utility apps, an AI content channel, and a SaaS cold outreach tool. Based in London. Open to junior and graduate developer roles at companies that move fast and ship real things.
